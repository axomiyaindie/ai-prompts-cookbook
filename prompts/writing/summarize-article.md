# Executive Article Summarizer

**Platform:** Any modern LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
**Category:** Writing
**Use Case:** Turn a long article into a concise, actionable summary for a busy professional.

## The Prompt
> Summarize the following article for a busy executive.
>
> Requirements:
> - Start with a one-sentence "Bottom Line" summary.
> - Follow with 3-5 bullet points of the key takeaways.
> - End with a "So What?" section explaining the practical implication.
> - Keep the total length under 150 words.
>
> Article:
> {{ARTICLE_TEXT}}

## Why It Works
It specifies the audience, the exact output format, and forces the model to add practical value beyond a plain summary.
