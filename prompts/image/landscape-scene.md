# Landscape Scene with Defined Mood

**Platform:** Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Imagen
**Category:** Image Generation
**Use Case:** Generate a landscape with a specific time of day, weather, and mood.

## The Prompt
> A wide landscape of {{LOCATION}} at {{TIME_OF_DAY}}, {{WEATHER}}, {{MOOD}} atmosphere, foreground detail, distant mountains, volumetric light, 24mm lens, high dynamic range, no people, no text.

## Why It Works
Landscape models respond to light and lens vocabulary. Pinning time of day, weather, and mood removes the usual ambiguity that produces generic output.
