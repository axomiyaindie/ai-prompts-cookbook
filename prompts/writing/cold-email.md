# Short Personalized Cold Email

**Platform:** ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini
**Category:** Writing
**Use Case:** Write a short, specific outreach email that does not read like a template.

## The Prompt
> Write a cold email to {{RECIPIENT_ROLE}} at {{COMPANY}} about {{OFFER}}.
>
> Rules:
> - Maximum 120 words.
> - Open with a specific observation about the recipient or company, not a generic compliment.
> - One clear ask in the final line.
> - No buzzwords, no exclamation marks.
> - Subject line under 8 words.
>
> Context:
> {{CONTEXT}}

## Why It Works
The word limit and the "specific observation" requirement force concrete personalization instead of filler that recipients ignore.
