# Portrait with Cinematic Lighting

**Platform:** Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Imagen
**Category:** Image Generation
**Use Case:** Generate a portrait with a specific photographic style.

## The Prompt
> A photo of {{SUBJECT}}, shot from a low angle, with dramatic side lighting, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, bokeh background.

## Why It Works
Image models respond to photography vocabulary (lens type, angle, lighting) rather than commands. This gives the model specific visual constraints to work with.
