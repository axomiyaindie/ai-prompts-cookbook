# Generate a Structured Outline

**Platform:** ChatGPT / Claude
**Category:** Writing
**Use Case:** Turn a topic and audience into a usable outline.

## The Prompt
> Create an outline for a {{LENGTH}}-word piece on {{TOPIC}} for {{AUDIENCE}}.
>
> Output:
> - A working title.
> - Three to five top-level sections.
> - Two to three bullet points per section.
> - A one-line thesis statement at the top.
>
> Constraints:
> - No jargon unless defined.
> - Each section should map to one reader question.

## Why It Works
Mapping sections to reader questions forces a reader-centric structure instead of a generic list of topics.
