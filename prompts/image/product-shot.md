# Product Shot on Clean Background

**Platform:** Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Imagen
**Category:** Image Generation
**Use Case:** Generate a clean, e-commerce style product image.

## The Prompt
> Product photo of {{PRODUCT}}, centered on a seamless {{COLOR}} background, soft studio lighting, subtle reflection, sharp focus, 50mm lens, high detail, no text, no watermark.

## Why It Works
E-commerce images depend on controlled lighting and background. Photography terms plus explicit negatives produce a consistent, usable result.
