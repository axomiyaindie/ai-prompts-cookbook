# Meeting Transcript to Decisions and Actions

**Platform:** ChatGPT / Claude
**Category:** Writing
**Use Case:** Turn a raw transcript into a clean summary with decisions and owners.

## The Prompt
> Convert the transcript below into meeting notes.
>
> Output:
> 1. Decisions made (bulleted, each with the rationale in one line).
> 2. Action items (table: owner, action, due date if stated).
> 3. Open questions.
>
> Rules:
> - Do not invent owners or dates. Use "unassigned" or "not stated".
> - Keep the total under 250 words.
>
> Transcript:
> {{TRANSCRIPT}}

## Why It Works
Separating decisions, actions, and open questions matches how teams actually use notes, and the no-invention rule keeps the output trustworthy.
